02 / Founder journey

My first startup
didn’t work out.
I kept building.

I founded Robovision Pvt Ltd to build educational hardware. Our product, Kit-E-Kit, combined a hands-on STEM hardware kit with AI-supported, game-based learning software.

The work included an IoT-enabled STEM learning kit with an educational drone. Robovision received ₹5 lakh in investment from MeitY for this venture.

The startup ultimately failed. It remains an important part of my founder journey—and the foundation I’m building on as I pursue new products.
